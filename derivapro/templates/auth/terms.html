Terms and Disclaimer

DerivaPro is an analytical application for research, education, and internal evaluation workflows.

Permitted Use

DerivaPro is provided for personal research, education, model exploration, and internal evaluation. The application is not intended for production trading, regulated decision-making, client advice, commercial redistribution, or external publication without independent validation and appropriate governance review.

No Advice

DerivaPro does not provide financial, investment, trading, accounting, tax, legal, valuation, or risk-management advice. Results are model-based estimates that depend on user inputs, assumptions, market data quality, and implementation limitations.

User Responsibility

Users are responsible for validating assumptions, model selection, calibration inputs, market data, numerical settings, and output interpretation. Do not rely on DerivaPro outputs as the sole basis for trading, hedging, reporting, regulatory, accounting, tax, or client-facing decisions.

Data Use

Registration information is collected to support user-specific workflows such as saved pricing runs, portfolios, reports, and analysis history. Do not submit confidential client data, material non-public information, or restricted data unless the deployment environment has been reviewed and approved for that use.
