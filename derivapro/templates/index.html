Purpose

Built for disciplined valuation workflows, not one-off calculators.

The platform is intended to help users compare pricing methods, inspect assumptions, evaluate payoff behavior, and build a repeatable path from individual instrument analysis toward portfolio-level risk management. The current app includes working pricing modules and the foundation for saved analysis, portfolio tracking, reporting, and model governance workflows.

Saved analysis path

Who should use it

Designed for quantitative finance learning, prototyping, and internal evaluation.

DerivaPro is most useful for students, researchers, model developers, risk analysts, product specialists, and finance professionals who want to inspect pricing mechanics and prototype analytics workflows in a browser.

How to use it

Start with a preview, then sign in for the full workspace.

Anonymous users can review the platform overview and try the European option preview. Registered users can access the broader product workspace, enter instrument assumptions, run analytics, and build toward saved analysis and reporting workflows.

Important use limitation

Research and evaluation only.

DerivaPro is provided for personal research, education, prototyping, and internal evaluation. It is not financial, investment, trading, accounting, tax, legal, or regulated risk-management advice. Outputs must be independently validated before any production, client-facing, commercial, or trading use.
