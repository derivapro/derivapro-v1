Swaptions

Access swaption valuation workflows and review analytical and mark-to-market coverage for interest rate optionality.

Description

A swaption provides the right, but not the obligation, to enter a swap at a future date. Use payer/receiver structures to evaluate convex exposure to rate moves, volatility assumptions, and tenor selection.

Instructions

1. For analytical pricing now, open the Swaptions Analytical workflow from Products.

2. Use this section as the launch point for expanded M2M swaption analytics as they are rolled out.

3. Align strike, expiry, tenor, and curve assumptions with your swaps risk workflows for consistency.
