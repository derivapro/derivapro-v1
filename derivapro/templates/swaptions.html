Swaption Analytics - Planned

This planned workflow will provide expanded mark-to-market, sensitivity, and scenario analytics for interest rate optionality.

Description

A swaption provides the right, but not the obligation, to enter a swap at a future date. Use payer/receiver structures to evaluate convex exposure to rate moves, volatility assumptions, and tenor selection.

Current Availability

1. For pricing now, open the European Swaption Pricing workflow.

2. Expanded mark-to-market and scenario analytics are planned but are not yet available on this page.

3. Once implemented, the workflow will align strike, expiry, tenor, volatility, and curve assumptions with related swap risk analysis.
