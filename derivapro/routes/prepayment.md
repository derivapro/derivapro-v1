**Description:**
Prepayment models estimate the probability that a borrower prepays a loan before contractual maturity. In mortgage and loan analytics, prepayment behavior is typically driven by refinancing incentive (rate spread), borrower credit quality, seasoning (loan age), and collateral leverage (LTV).

This page provides a configurable probability calculator based on a logistic-form setup, allowing users to test how key borrower and market drivers influence expected prepayment behavior.

**Instructions:**
Use this page to compute prepayment probability under custom assumptions.

1. Enter borrower and loan features (original rate, current market rate, FICO, loan age, and LTV).
2. Enter model coefficients (intercept and factor betas) consistent with your calibration.
3. Run the calculation to produce prepayment probability output.
4. Adjust assumptions to perform scenario testing (rate shocks, borrower profile changes).
5. Use the resulting probabilities as inputs to cash flow, valuation, or risk forecasting workflows.
