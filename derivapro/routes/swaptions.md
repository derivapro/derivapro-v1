**Description:**
A swaption is an option granting the right, but not the obligation, to enter an interest rate swap at a future exercise date. Payer swaptions provide the right to pay fixed/receive floating, while receiver swaptions provide the right to receive fixed/pay floating.

Conceptually, swaption value depends on:

* underlying forward swap rate,
* strike rate,
* expiry and underlying tenor,
* volatility and discounting assumptions.

This workflow supports valuation and scenario analysis for mark-to-market swaption use cases.
